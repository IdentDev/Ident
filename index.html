Sharp, Gradient & Arrow

Sharp Transition (Default): Indicates that multiple identities or orientations coexist simultaneously in your life.

Gradient Transition: Represents a fluid identity that shifts, rotates, or fluctuates across different periods of time (ideal for Abrosexual, Genderfluid, or Bi-flux folk).

Arrow Transition: Shows two, three or more orientations subordinate to one. Put the umbrella orientation first and the specific orientations it covers after it; the arrow points from the umbrella into them. For example, Bisexual does not always mean both homosexual and heterosexual, so for full inclusivity follow it with the exact orientations you mean.

Inside a group: orientations gathered under one umbrella are linked to each other with arrows too, so the group reads as one unit and stays distinct from a separate identity beside it. Example: Bi (Hetero + Cetero) together with Demi is Bi → Hetero → Cetero | Demi: arrow, arrow, then a Sharp edge before Demi. Bi → Hetero | Cetero | Demi would read as four unrelated identities.

Across time (e.g. Abrosexual): when your orientation differs from one period of life to another, put a Gradient between the periods. Example: in one period you are Bi (Hetero + Cetero) and Demi at the same time, in another you are Homo: Bi → Hetero → Cetero | Demi / Homo, where “/” is the Gradient. Read the Gradients first: they split the picture into moments in time. Inside each moment, arrows group an umbrella with the orientations it covers, and Sharp edges separate what coexists.
